# Worked sheets

Three sheets built from real sources. Read one when a case here matches what you are holding.

## Sausage and Peppers Stew — `sausage-stew-sheet.html`

15 ingredients, 7 operations, two branches, one page at 5 method columns.
Source: Deep South Dish, 2015.

Tests the **short-and-wide** shape and operation merging. The source's steps imply nine
operations; two of them — *set aside to cool slightly* and *bring to a boil* — have no stop of
their own and fold into the operations they precede, which is what brings the tree inside the fit
test. Also: a timing conflict stated in the band (the source claims 25 minutes of cooking against
27–32 in the skillet alone), a tied seasoning with an open total, and one duration the source
defers to a sausage package.

## World Peace Cookies — `world-peace-cookies-sheet.html`

9 ingredients, 10 operations, one long chain with a short branch, two pages.
Source: Dorie Greenspan, *Dorie's Cookies*.

Tests **tall-and-thin**, staging, and cues without clocks. The chill sits at the foot of page 1
because the cook leaves during it. The dough's stop is *big, moist curds*, never a time — the
author says outright that the dough varies batch to batch. Also: an operation repeated over a
batch (two logs, two sheets, sequential), an appliance step with no operands (the preheat, which
fits inside the chill), a source hedging one quantity four ways, and a typo — *bake the cookies 1
minute long* — fixed silently because only one reading is viable.

## Instant Pot Sausage and Peppers — `instant-pot-sausage-peppers-sheet.html`

10 ingredients, 6 operations, one page at 4 method columns.
Source: personal shorthand notes.

Tests **an incomplete source**. Olive oil, broth and salt appear in the method and not the
ingredient list. Rice appears only in the title, has no parent operation, and belongs in the band
— the grid finds it by producing a row one cell too wide. Four marked inferences, all genuinely
unresolvable: pepper count, garlic cut, browning time, and the pot's minimum liquid. Also a
two-product step, where the sausages come out and the fond stays in, with the link stated at the
deglaze rather than drawn.

## What they are for

They are precedents, not templates. A rule that suits the stew can break the cookies, which is why
all three stay here. When a change to this skill is proposed, run it against all three before
accepting it — every one of them has already caught a defect that reasoning alone did not.
